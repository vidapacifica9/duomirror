# Privacy Policy for DuoMirror

**Last Updated:** September 2026

At DuoMirror, your privacy is our highest priority. This policy outlines our straightforward approach to data privacy.

---

### 1. No Data Collection
DuoMirror does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, telemetry, usage statistics, or identifiers.

### 2. Local Point-to-Point Video Streaming
All video transmission between the host iPhone and your viewing device (via the web browser) occurs exclusively over your local network (Wi-Fi or Personal Hotspot). 
* Video data **never** travels across the public internet or external cloud servers.
* No video frames, screenshots, or camera streams are ever recorded, saved, or uploaded.

### 3. Device Permissions
* **Camera (`NSCameraUsageDescription`):** Used exclusively to capture the live video feed streamed to your viewing screen.
* **Local Network (`NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription`):** Used strictly to discover and broadcast the local WebSocket feed directly to your second device on the same local subnet.

### 4. Third-Party Services
DuoMirror contains zero third-party tracking SDKs, analytics frameworks, or advertising libraries.

### 5. Contact
If you have any questions or feedback regarding this policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
